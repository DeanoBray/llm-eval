Three ways information goes missing

Omission

A response that is technically accurate but leaves out major context the question actually needed.

Self-redaction

A model narrows or softens its own answer on a topic it can address, without being asked to.

Training bias

Gaps that trace back to what the model saw during training, not a deliberate choice at answer time.
