LLM Bias Evaluation

Measuring cross-cultural and cross-lingual bias in large language models

IDP_OL_LLMEval_G6 · La Trobe University

About the Project

Large language models are shaped by the data, values, and governance of the countries where they're developed. This project evaluates how US and Chinese LLMs respond to politically sensitive topics — in both English and Mandarin — measuring refusal rates, factual accuracy, and framing bias.
